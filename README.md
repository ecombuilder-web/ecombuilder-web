## What we do

Most companies don't need another AI tool. They need AI that fits how their business already runs. **Triple Digit** looks at how your team actually works, then designs and builds the AI around it.

- 🤖 **Custom AI agents** that do real work in your business: support, research, operations, reporting.
- ⚙️ **Workflow automation** across the tools your team already uses: email, CRM, documents, spreadsheets and internal systems.
- 🧠 **Claude Code and agentic coding setups**: MCP connectors, skills and mods tuned to your team and your codebase.
- 🖥️ **Custom Claude Desktop panels** that put the information your team needs beside every conversation.
- 🌐 **AI-powered websites and internal tools**, built, shipped and kept running.

Every project starts from your company's own process and data, not from a template.
